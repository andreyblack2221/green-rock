## Challenges & Growth Areas
- **The "Comparison Tax":** Aligning ML results with the baseline proved technically difficult, especially regarding temporal indexing.
- **Misleading Indicators:** Initial implementation of `st.metric` deltas was semantically incorrect (always green), requiring a patch.
- **Testing Gaps:** E2E tests initially lacked validation for visual metrics rendering, focusing too much on backend logic.
- **UI Resilience:** Early iterations of Act 2 were "brittle"—one model failure could crash the entire view.

## Key Insights & Learnings
- **Service Layer Sanctity:** Direct calls from Entrypoints to Domain logic create "spaghetti" that breaks hexagonal architecture. Always route through the Service Layer.
- **Semantic Colors Matter:** In financial dashboards, color direction (green/red) must reflect actual value change, not just absolute magnitude.
- **Mock Alignment:** E2E test mocks need to perfectly mirror the complex multi-model data structures returned by the pipeline.
